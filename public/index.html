What you'll discover

Four ways a brain gets clear enough to act.

Your result shows all four, not just one. Most people lead with one process and lean on a second. The one you're weakest in is usually where your misunderstandings start.

Green · Chaotic processing

Acts to understand

Starts with the big picture, tries things, and finds alternatives others miss.

Needs: room to test it. Frustrated by: long explanations before anything happens.

Red · Linear processing

Structures to understand

Needs logic, sequence and consistency. Spots the gap in the plan first.

Needs: clear steps. Frustrated by: changes nobody explained.

Purple · Relational processing

Connects the details

Gathers information, links it together, and weighs every option before committing.

Needs: detail and time. Frustrated by: decisions on the spot.

Blue · Intuitive processing

Senses the meaning

Reads people, tone and patterns, and asks what the situation means for everyone.

Needs: the why and the people impact. Frustrated by: cold, purely logical plans.

What you get

Not a color. A pattern you can actually use.

Your result shows how strongly each process runs in you, which one leads, which one backs it up, and the one that trips you up with other people. Then it tells you what to do about it.

  • Your four scores, with intensity. A light lead means flexibility; a dark lead means the trait is strong.
  • Your miscommunication zone: the process you find hardest to read, and who is likely to feel misunderstood by you.
  • What to do differently with each of the other three colors, and what to say.
  • Your teammates, once you connect: who's in whose danger zone, before it costs you a project.

FAQ

Before you start.

What is the CBCI?

The Colored Brain Communication Inventory is an assessment rooted in Directive Communication Psychology. It measures how your brain gets clarity and interprets information. It isn't a behavior or personality test.

How long does it take?

About 10 to 15 minutes. 20 questions in three sections. Find a quiet spot and answer with your first instinct.

My coach sent me a code. What do I do?

Click "I have a code from my coach", enter the email your coach used and the 8-character code, choose your language, and start.

Are there right or wrong answers?

No. Answer based on how you naturally process, not how you've learned to behave or what you think is "correct".

Which languages can I take it in?

English, Malay, Russian, Indonesian, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi and Sinhala.

Who sees my results?

You, and the coach who gave you your code. If you join a team, teammates see what the team's sharing settings allow.

Do we need a trainer to roll this out to a team?

No. A leader can buy codes, share the team code and read the team dashboard the same day. Certified trainers add facilitated debriefs and workshops when you want them.

How long does a team rollout take?

Most teams finish within a week: buy codes today, everyone takes 15 minutes when it suits them, results appear as they complete.

Do I need the AI to get my results?

No. Your CBCI results are included with the assessment. The AI plans add coaching, pair guidance and team tools on top.
