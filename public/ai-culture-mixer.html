How to tell which one you are (without the sliders)

  1. Listen to your own first sentence the next time AI gets something wrong. "Who did this?" means Judge.
  2. Ask who owns AI on your team. If the answer is a department, you are an Outsourcer.
  3. Ask when YOU last used AI in front of your people. Can't remember? Spectator.
  4. Ask what happens when you are away for two weeks. If AI projects stop, you are a Hero. If they speed up, you are a Leader Maker.
