The Colored Brain system now

People are not difficult. They are predictable, once you have the right three inputs.

Colored Brain never tried to explain everything about a person. It answered one usable question: how does this person get clear enough to act? That is still the foundation. The new system adds two layers on top.

LAYER 1

Clarity Process

The CBCI identifies how each person processes information to reach clarity: Green, Red, Purple or Blue. This is where most trust breaks start, because leaders read a different clarity process as resistance, attitude or incompetence.

LAYER 2

Emotional Drives

The 8 Emotional Drives explain what each person is actually working for: Belonging, Security, Diversity, Recognition, Achievement, Growth, Excellence, Contribution. Starve someone's top drive and their behaviour changes, whatever their brain colour. The $10 Million Question maps them in two minutes.

LAYER 3

AI Prediction

The Predictive Psychology engine combines clarity process and drives for every member of a specific team and forecasts how each person will react to a given situation: a moved deadline, a new manager, a reorg. Not a type. A forecast for this team, this week.
