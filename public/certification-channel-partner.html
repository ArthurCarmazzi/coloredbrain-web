Before you enrol

The fine print, in plain English.

Is this a one-off or a subscription?

One payment of $3,880. It covers your partner status, your back end, the bulk rates and the launch support. An installment plan of 6 x $697 is available if you would rather spread it.

What territory do I get?

Partners build in a region or a niche. The specifics are agreed with the DCI team as part of launch support rather than sold off a web page.

Do I need to be a Trainer first?

No. Channel Partner includes everything in Trainer and Practitioner.

Is the certification accredited?

Yes. Channel Partner is accredited by the American Institute of Business Psychology, as is Trainer level. Practitioner is not.

Can I certify Practitioners immediately?

You get the rights on day one. In practice most partners run their own certification once they have delivered a few programs themselves, and the launch support covers that sequencing.

What happens in the annual strategy session?

A working session with the DCI team on your pipeline, your pricing and what to build next in your market.
