The part that keeps working after you leave the room.

ChatGPT gives every client the same advice because it knows nothing about them. Predictive Psychology AI reads each person's actual CBCI result, adds their assessed motivators, then works the real situation in front of them. Before you enrol

The fine print, in plain English.

Do I need to have taken the CBCI first?

No. Your own CBCI is included, and so is a second code so you can practice on a real person. If you have already taken it, your existing result carries over.

How long does it take?

The modules are self-paced, so it depends on you. The weekly live sessions with the DCI team run continuously, and you join the next one after you enrol.

Can I use Colored Brain with clients without certifying?

Yes. You can buy CBCI codes and AI seats and use them in your practice today. Certification adds the debrief skill, the method in depth, and the right to present yourself as certified.

What happens after the included AI month ends?

Your clients' completed CBCI results stay on their accounts. You choose whether to continue an AI plan for yourself or put clients on their own.

Can I upgrade to Trainer later?

Talk to the DCI team about credit for what you have already paid. We will not pretend there is an automatic policy here, because the honest answer is that it is handled case by case.
